About

I'm a product designer, and the thread through my work is making complex systems legible — enterprise software where the domain is genuinely hard and the interface still has to be clear under pressure. I work analytically and user-first, I make and defend the decisions behind the screens, and I ship — I was the sole designer on a secure document system through a full release, and everything I designed was approved and built.

That user-first instinct is older than my time in tech. I trained as a marketer and led brand and creative teams for six years, where you learn to start from the audience: to know exactly who you're building for before you build. After a decade directing and collaborating with designers, leading the work comes naturally. I design in Figma with AI in the loop, and I'm looking for a senior product design role — remote or based in Warsaw.
